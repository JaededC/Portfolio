About Me

From kitchens to business systems

I'm an MIS student at SUNY Old Westbury with a habit of looking at how things work and wondering how they could work better. Before moving into technology, I studied culinary arts and worked in restaurants, where clear communication and reliable processes mattered every day.

Now I work as a Data Analyst Intern in Old Westbury's Office of Institutional Effectiveness and as a STEM Instructor at Morrison Mentors. My projects connect those experiences: understanding a practical problem, figuring out what people need, and learning the tools to build something useful.

I'm interested in business and systems analysis, especially work that makes everyday processes easier for the people using them.

What I work with

Systems analysis
Requirements gathering, stakeholder analysis, AS-IS and TO-BE process mapping, gap analysis, and relational modeling.
Data and reporting
SQL, Power BI, Excel, Power Query, data cleaning, validation, and visualization.
Workflow tools
Power Apps, Power Automate, SharePoint, and Microsoft 365.
